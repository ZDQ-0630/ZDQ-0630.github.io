01 / The proposition

Tailored for the
way work moves.

The concept brings together an office-ready silhouette and a more comfortable experience for Singapore's sun and heat.

01

Professional form

Refined shapes designed for meetings, commuting and client-facing days.

02

Sun-conscious fabric

UV protection is a product goal. The final claim will depend on fabric and garment testing.

03

Personal fit

Made-to-order options aim to give customers more control over fit and style.

02 / Business model

One design idea.
Several ways to buy.

Individual orders build the collection. Corporate orders and gifting open a second route to professional customers.

01

Individual orders

Customers select a style and provide measurements through a planned digital ordering experience.

02

Corporate clothing

Companies could commission coordinated workwear that reflects their brand and employees' needs.

03

Corporate gifting

Garments and gift cards could introduce the collection to employees, clients and their networks.

Proposed individual price range: S$80–180. Pricing is an assumption in the group concept, not a live store offer.

Visibility through genuine wear.

A proposed outreach route is to invite respected figures in business communities to try the clothing. Any recommendation would depend on their own experience; a gifted or sponsored post would disclose that relationship.

A second route to market

A uniform
that still feels
individual.

Corporate workwear can share a visual identity while allowing employees to choose a suitable fit and silhouette. This is a proposed offer for future company pilots.
