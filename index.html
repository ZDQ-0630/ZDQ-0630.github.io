04 / Business model

How AUREVA
could earn.

Three proposed revenue routes, with one focused launch sequence. None represents signed contracts or recorded sales.

01

Individual orders

Sell selected styles via the brand site and marketplaces, beginning with a limited range and small production runs.

02

Corporate workwear

Pitch a paid uniform pilot to one employer with agreed quantity, size mix and alteration policy.

03

Gifting & referrals

Test gift cards and disclosed product seeding through business associations and trusted professional voices.

A practical first pilot

  1. Choose one fabric and two silhouettes; obtain supplier quotations and test plans.
  2. Fit-test samples with professional women and revise patterns.
  3. Run a small paid B2C batch and seek one corporate brief.
  4. Measure returns, margin and buyer feedback before expanding.

AI body measurement and virtual try-on are later-stage ideas. This site has a visual configurator, not a fit tool.

06 / Evidence plan

A clear path
to proof.

The concept and visual prototype are ready to show. Performance, demand and venture economics still need real tests.

Supported context

  • MOM reports resident women's labour-force participation at ages 25–64 rose from 74.1% in 2015 to 80.5% in 2025.
  • NEA says very high and extreme UV Index levels are common in Singapore between 11am and 3pm on lightly clouded days.
  • The team has a collection concept, brand identity and interactive visual prototype.

Validation gates

  • Independent fabric and garment UPF tests, including wear and wash checks.
  • Supplier quotations, minimum order quantity, sample quality and real unit costs.
  • Customer interviews, paid pilot orders and corporate buyer feedback.
  • Named team roles, signed partners and any IP filings before claiming defensibility.
